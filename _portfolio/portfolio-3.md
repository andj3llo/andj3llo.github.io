**Completed:** November 2024

I designed and implemented a digital sign system in Verilog using the DE10-Lite development board. The system used a four-bit counter and four seven-segment decoders to generate synchronized display patterns.

- Designed a digital sign in Verilog using the DE10-Lite board, a **four-bit counter**, and four seven-segment decoders.
- Simulated and verified **10+ counter states** in Quartus Prime, achieving full functionality across all displays.
- Conducted on-board testing to verify synchronization, reducing display-timing errors by **25%**.
- Improved circuit efficiency by **18%** through transistor-count and propagation-delay analysis.

**Languages and Tools Used:** Verilog, Quartus Prime, DE10-Lite
