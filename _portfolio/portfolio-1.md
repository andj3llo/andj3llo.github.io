In a year-long collaborative project, I tackled the issue of sleep deprivation caused by video games. Through extensive research and the application of the engineering design process, I developed a makeshift controller and a phone application to address this challenge. Throughout the project, I delivered comprehensive presentations at key milestones, showcasing our progress and the effectiveness of our solutions.

**Languages Used:** Arduino IDE, MIT App Inventor, Processing
